## A9 — hosted deployment (done)

Shipped: `src/deploy/runtime.py` (the logic the notebooks share),
`deploy/notebooks/{colab,kaggle}.ipynb` (generated by
`scripts/build_notebooks.py`), `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`, and three test modules
(77 tests). `TEMP_DIR`/`OUTPUT_DIR` became environment-overridable so a notebook
can redirect the runtime tree off the checkout.

What is **not** validated here, and why: the notebooks were exercised on a CPU
box (platform detection, runtime redirect, a real server start/health/stop), but
the Colab and Kaggle *paths themselves* — the `google.colab.kernel.proxyPort`
call, the `apt-get` install, the CUDA extras — cannot be run without those
accounts. The notebook logic is therefore tested structurally and the helpers it
calls are tested directly; the platform-specific cells remain the untested part.
Closing this needs one real Colab run and one real Kaggle run.

## Phase 7 validation gaps (cannot be closed in this environment)

These are the only parts of Phase 7 that are validated at the mechanism level
rather than on rendered output.

1. **Lip-sync inference is not run.** This environment has no Wav2Lip
   (`models/wav2lip/wav2lip_gan.pth`) or LivePortrait inference path usable
   end to end. The detector and placement policy are validated on real frames
   (16/49 single-face eligible, 33 multi-face untouched), but no frame with a
   moved mouth was produced. Closing this needs the Wav2Lip checkpoint and a
   GPU run.
2. **HIGH preset enhancement is unverified.** `enable_audio_enhancement=True`
   depends on `voicefixer`, which is not installed here. The preset is applied
   and asserted, but the enhanced audio was never heard. Needs
   `pip install voicefixer` plus a short render.
3. **Single-face eligibility is loose.** `_detect_frame_boxes` treats any
   one-face frame as eligible without a confidence or size check, so a tiny or
   spurious detection is still lip-synced. A minimum-face-size or
   landmark-confidence gate would tighten this. Not a regression, a refinement.

## Deferred hardening (Phase 6 audit, still open)

Documented as known limits when Phase 6 shipped. None is a live bug under the
single-process contract; all matter before multi-user or server use.

1. **Cross-process locking.** Nothing stops two concurrent runs on one job from
   interleaving manifest saves. A `JobLock` (`O_CREAT|O_EXCL` plus
   stale-takeover) was prototyped and deliberately left out. Do this before any
   multi-user deployment.
2. **Retry hooks without a caller.** `should_retry_segment`, `summarise`, and
   `RetryOutcome.history` in `src/core/retry.py` are tested but have no
   production caller. Either wire per-segment retry or remove them.
3. **Config drift reconciliation.** A finished job is now reconciled through
   `Manifest.differs_from` / `reconcile` for allow-listed (sticky) changes. A
   disallowed change still refuses with `JobError`; it does not yet reconcile
   the affected upstream stages and continue. Decide whether upstream
   reconciliation should ever be automatic.

## Candidate Phase 8 (proposal, not started)

If a new phase is opened, this is the natural scope, in priority order.

1. **Real lip-sync validation.** Bring the Wav2Lip checkpoint in, render the
   HGF scene with FAST and BALANCED, and measure identity drift and mouth
   sharpness against the source. This closes Phase 7 gap 1.
2. **Concurrency and locking.** Implement `JobLock`, make `Manifest.save`
   lock-aware, and add a two-runner test that proves one writer waits or
   declines. This closes deferred item 1 and is the prerequisite for a server.
3. **Speaker-aware multi-face handling.** Replace "leave it alone" with
   diarization-guided selection: lip-sync the active speaker when the speaker
   and the face can be associated with confidence, and fall back to
   pass-through only when they cannot. Builds on the Phase 3 speaker
   references and the Phase 7 detector output.
4. **Per-segment TTS quality gate.** Use the retry hooks (deferred item 2) to
   re-synthesize a segment that is truncated, clipped, or far from the target
   duration before it reaches the mix.
5. **Enhancement path.** Verify and, if needed, fix `enable_audio_enhancement`
   under the HIGH preset (gap 2).

## How to reproduce the Phase 7 evidence

- Full suite: `env PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/ -q`
  (1059 passed, 14 skipped at time of writing).
- Phase 7 focused: `tests/test_phase7_scoping_lipsync_mix.py` and
  `tests/test_phase7_real_validation.py`.
- Real-sample inputs: `jobs/hgf_scene-20261021-142210-f20a32a1/` (stems under
  `separation/`, timeline under `sync/segments.json`) and the sample clips in
  `samples/`.
- The merged dub and final mix are under `temp/` and the rendered result is
  `output/translated_hgf_scene.webm`.
